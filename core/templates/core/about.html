About VARIANZA

Leadership grounded in students, strengthened by action.

VARIANZA is a student leadership slate of the Organization of Statistics Students committed to building a more connected, transparent, academically supportive, and responsive student organization.

Who We Are

VARIANZA is a commitment to representation.

The Organization of Statistics Students exists to bring Statistics students together as a community. VARIANZA seeks to strengthen that community by making student leadership more accessible, accountable, and responsive.

We believe leadership should not be measured only by titles or promises. It should be seen through consistent communication, responsible stewardship, meaningful programs, and the willingness to listen to the students we represent.

Our goal is to help build an OSS where students are informed, supported in their academic journey, given opportunities to grow, and encouraged to actively participate in the organization.

Our Purpose

Turning student voices into meaningful action.

VARIANZA aims to create an environment where students can communicate their concerns, contribute ideas, access academic support, and see tangible action from their elected representatives.

01

Representation

Creating channels where students can express concerns, ideas, and perspectives—and ensuring those voices are heard.

02

Transparency

Promoting clear communication, responsible documentation, and accountability in organizational decisions and resources.

03

Academic Growth

Building opportunities for peer learning, practical Statistics, research, collaboration, and continuous academic development.

04

Action

Moving beyond ideas by developing programs and systems that respond to real student needs.

Our Approach

Listen. Build. Improve.

We approach student leadership as an ongoing process of listening to the community, building practical solutions, and evaluating their impact.

01

Listen

Understand the concerns, experiences, and ideas of Statistics students.

02

Build

Translate those needs into programs, systems, and opportunities that students can actually use.

03

Improve

Measure results, gather feedback, and continuously improve what we do for the organization.

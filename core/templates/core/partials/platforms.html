Our Platforms

Plans grounded in student needs

The platforms of VARIANZA are built around academic growth, transparent governance, student representation, and meaningful opportunities for the Statistics community.

Individual Platforms

Our candidates' commitments

United and Purpose-Driven Leadership

Leadership comes with clear communication. I aim to create a healthy and united planning and executive process that allows the voices of the student body and its constituents to be heard and acted upon.

Transparent and Hands-On Presentation

The student body should provide complete and transparent documentation of meetings, activities, and funds to the organization. The students under OSS should be informed of the actions, progress, and activities of the elected officers.

Healthy Collaborative Learning

I aim to create a space to assist struggling students through creation of learning materials to supplement learning gaps. I want to target key fundamental concepts that students may struggle to comprehend and apply in their studies.

Student Grievance & Project Pipeline

A dedicated communication channel where students can openly raise concerns and feedback regarding our organization, while also serving as an active hub where they can share ideas and suggestions to help us build meaningful future projects for the department.

Internal Leadership Empowerment

A comprehensive training program featuring specialized orientations and leadership seminars for all organization officers and committee members, designed to equip them with the right skills, guidance, and direction so they can seamlessly manage internal operations and lead our department with excellence.

Streamlined Department Communications

A reliable and centralized messaging network that ensures all important announcements, schedules, and departmental updates reach every Statistics student clearly, quickly, and without confusion.

Making OSS an Academically Healthy Competitive Environment

I want to promote a healthy competitive environment within OSS where students are encouraged to challenge themselves, improve their skills, and strive for excellence. Competition should motivate students while remaining fair, merit-based, and supportive.

Meritocracy and Healthy Competition

I advocate for giving students opportunities based on their effort, skills, and performance. Competition should not put students against one another, but encourage everyone to improve, reach higher standards, and support their fellow students.

Practical Learning and Professional Experience

I advocate for opportunities where students can apply Statistics to actual situations. Working with real data, problems, and organizations can help students gain practical experience and confidence even before graduation.

Building a Culture of Knowledge

I advocate for preserving and sharing academic work produced by our Statistics community. Through a Digital Library, students can learn from previous research and continue building on the knowledge created by past batches.

CONNECT

To improve the flow of information within the organization by creating a more organized and accessible way of sharing announcements, reminders, schedules, and important updates. Through this platform, not only will members stay informed, but Statistics students will also be informed about the organization's activities and opportunities.

SECure Information

To strengthen the documentation and record-keeping of our organization by properly organizing important files, meeting minutes, event records, and achievements. The goal is to be a reliable repository for the organization's files that preserves organizational memory and helps future officers continue and improve the work of those before them.

STAT-VOICE

This platform gives every Statistics student an opportunity to share their opinion, suggestions, and concerns through simple surveys and feedback forms. This will help the organization understand what students need and want, making future activities more student-centered and data-informed.

Organized Information, Inclusive Participation

I advocate that every student is informed, heard, and allowed to participate in activities organized by the organization. Good communication and proper organization can help create a stronger community, while listening to our members can help us make better decisions and plan activities for everyone.

Secure & Private Records

I will keep your records safe and private. Sensitive data will be stored properly, with backups maintained to ensure that important files remain protected and accessible when needed.

Order and Better Results

I believe real progress starts with how things are organized and how we work each day. As Assistant Secretary, I will help maintain steady operations through clear notes, accurate records, and messages that are easy to understand.

Steady Help for the Team

As Assistant Secretary, I will support the Secretary and the entire organization in a dependable and responsible manner. I will carry out my duties carefully and on time, with accuracy and consistency as priorities.

POWER OF YOUR VOICE

Your voice has real weight. Ideas should not only be gathered. They should be checked, talked through, and used. What you share should help shape rules and improve life in the community. Small opinions still count, and every person should have a chance to be heard.

Every Peso Accounted For

To provide complete transparency and responsibility in handling the organization's funds. Every contribution, expense, and remaining balance will be recorded, documented, and reported, with every financial decision made in the organization's best interests.

Making Every Peso Count

This focuses on using the organization's funds wisely and purposefully. Every peso collected through fundraising, donations, membership fees, and other sources should contribute to the organization's goals and benefit its members.

Putting Every Peso Toward Learning

This prioritizes spending on activities and resources that directly support the academic growth of members. Every peso entrusted to the organization should contribute to students' growth and create better opportunities for learning.

Regular Auditing

A regular review of financial records will be conducted to examine transactions and verify their supporting documents in compliance with the organization's policies. This aims to ensure that records are accurate, complete, and properly documented to avoid discrepancies.

Monthly and Annual Financial Reporting

A clear and comprehensive financial report will be presented and made accessible to students. This aims to promote transparency and provide the Department of Statistics student body with a better understanding of how organizational funds are allocated and utilized.

Organizational Feedback and Evaluation

A regular feedback form will be posted as an avenue for students to confidentially express their concerns, suggestions, and feedback regarding the organization's performance. This aims to encourage constructive engagement, strengthen accountability, and identify areas where the organization can improve its services.

Student Budget Wishlist

An open platform where students can submit suggestions on how organization funds should be allocated for programs, resources, and projects. The top suggestions will be reviewed and presented before events. This aims to involve students in financial decision-making.

Data Audit Literacy

An educational initiative that explains financial terms and processes used in organizational auditing. Through posts and guides, students learn how to read audit reports, track expenses, and understand budget categories based on real organizational documents.

Post-Event Financial Tracker

A reporting system that publishes the actual expenses of every major event. Each tracker shows the approved budget versus actual spending with charts, posted within one week after the event.

Transparent Data, Accountable Leadership

An advocacy for financial transparency and accountability. By making audit data clear and accessible, students are informed and supported in upholding responsible fund management.

Project Impact Check

This establishes an evaluation system for our projects to ensure that every activity is purposeful and responsive to student needs. Each project will begin with clear objectives and end with an evaluation based on participant feedback.

STAT SYNC: Peer Academic Support

To create a peer-led academic support program where Statistics students can collaborate and seek help with difficult concepts. Scheduled review sessions, student facilitators, volunteer tutors, practice problems, and reviewers will provide a supportive environment where students can learn from one another.

Income Generating Projects

To create sustainable income-generating projects throughout the academic year through affordable products and activities such as event booths, merchandise sales, and other student-friendly projects.

Statistics News Flash

To keep Statistics students informed through timely, accurate, and reliable updates regarding events and matters involving RTU students. This includes class suspensions, school occasions, organization activities, and other important notices.

Statisticians First

To give Statistics students a stronger voice on issues, activities, and events inside and outside the campus. I want to create more opportunities for students to express their concerns, preferences, suggestions, and opinions.

Strong Connections, Greater Opportunities

To encourage genuine connections among Statistics students. Strong relationships within the student community can open opportunities inside and outside the campus while helping students become more confident and willing to participate.

Clean Up, Stand Out!

Encourage a clean, healthy, and inspiring learning environment. Help students maintain cleanliness in every facility and provide hygiene goods such as tissues, sanitary pads, and hand soap where possible.

Earth Keeper

Ensure that no waste will be left behind after events. Keep areas clean during events and avoid materials that unnecessarily disrupt the environment.

Root Note

Posts regarding environmental challenges, cleanliness, pollution, the 5 R's, and related topics that remind students that our environment affects us and that small actions can make a difference.

Securing Our Environment: Protect Our Future

I advocate for an inspiring, uplifting environment where students are motivated to become the best versions of themselves. Ultimately, who our students become depends on the environment we build for them.

STAT-SUPPORT

Making Statistics Students' Lives More Connected, Supported, and Heard.

Academic Support

I aim to create a shared reviewer and resource bank for Statistics subjects while encouraging upper-year students to share tips, notes, and study strategies that can help fellow students.

Student Concerns & Representation

I want to establish a simple “You Speak, We Listen” system where students can anonymously submit concerns and suggestions. I also aim to regularly bring 2nd-year concerns to organization officers and faculty, while giving updates on what actions are taken.
